The person behind the project

The thinking behind SYNES.

SYNES began with a practical question: when an automated workflow fails, can the person supporting it follow the evidence, explain what happened, make a safe decision and show that recovery worked?

INDEPENDENTLY BUILT · DUBLIN, IRELAND

Meet the builder

I'm Arham. My background combines 2.5+ years in enterprise BI, ERP and reporting operations at Hitachi Digital Services with an MSc in Computing (Data Analytics) from Dublin City University.

That experience shaped how I approach technical problems: start with the evidence, isolate the failure, explain the options and verify the outcome. I created SYNES to put that way of thinking into an interactive environment that people can explore for themselves.

01 / WHY I BUILT SYNES

Make the work behind the workflow visible.

Automation is only useful when people can trust and support it. SYNES brings together a visual workflow builder, synthetic API failures, execution evidence, human approval checkpoints, recovery verification and an activity trail.

I wanted the experience to show more than a successful run. It should also show how someone investigates a failure, understands the uncertainty, decides what is safe to do and checks whether the recovery actually worked.

02 / WHAT YOU CAN EXPLORE

One project, several operational perspectives.

Build: create and connect workflow steps, configure nodes and simulate a run.

Investigate: inspect a failed step, review response evidence and explain a likely cause.

Decide: keep a human approval boundary before a proposed recovery action.

Verify: inspect the simulated result and retain an activity record.

Support: communicate the issue, impact, evidence, next action and outcome clearly.

Independence and boundaries

Support thinking for intelligent workflows.

SYNES is an independent educational portfolio project. It is not an official Tines product and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Tines. It does not connect to a Tines tenant or claim feature parity. The overlap is in the problem-solving approach: understand the workflow, inspect the evidence, keep people in control and verify the result.

All incidents, records and workflow runs shown here are synthetic. The demonstration does not perform real-world remediation or use real customer data.
